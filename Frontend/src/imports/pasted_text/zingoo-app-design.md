Overall Style

The final design should feel like a premium, cute, cozy social platform that is inspired by modern social media but has its own original identity. It should emphasize soft pastel aesthetics, rounded UI, friendly illustrations, delightful micro-interactions, and a polished user experience suitable for a brand called Zingoo.
